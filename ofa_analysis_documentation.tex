\section{Temporal Structure and Period Definition}
%==============================================================================

\subsection{Avoiding Simpson's Paradox}

A critical methodological concern in our analysis is \textbf{Simpson's Paradox}---the phenomenon where aggregate results can show different (or even opposite) trends compared to disaggregated subgroup analysis.

\subsubsection{The Problem}

Our initial analysis revealed that:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The data spans \textbf{four non-contiguous 8-day sampling periods}
    \item Market conditions (ETH price, volatility, gas) varied substantially across periods
    \item Combining all data would conflate time-varying market conditions with platform effects
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{The Solution}

We implement \textbf{period-stratified analysis}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item All analyses are conducted separately for each temporal period
    \item Cross-period comparisons use difference-in-differences (DiD) methodology
    \item We never pool data across periods without controlling for period fixed effects
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Outlier Detection: IQR Method}

We apply the Interquartile Range (IQR) method with 1.5$\times$IQR bounds:

\begin{align}
\text{Lower Bound} &= Q_1 - 1.5 \times IQR = -2.50 - 1.5 \times 14.20 = -23.80 \text{ bps} \\
\text{Upper Bound} &= Q_3 + 1.5 \times IQR = 11.70 + 1.5 \times 14.20 = +33.00 \text{ bps}
\end{align}

\textbf{Final filter applied}: $-24 \text{ bps} \leq \text{markout}_{60s} \leq +33 \text{ bps}$

\subsection{Justification for IQR Filter}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Statistical rigor}: IQR is distribution-based, not arbitrary
    \item \textbf{Retains 84.2\%}: Removes extreme outliers while preserving majority
    \item \textbf{Mean $\approx$ Median}: Post-filter distribution is approximately symmetric
    \item \textbf{Consistent with literature}: Standard practice in financial microstructure
\end{enumerate}

\section{Analysis Methodology}
%==============================================================================

\subsection{Primary Outcome Variable}

The primary outcome is the \textbf{60-second markout} ($M_{60}$), measuring execution quality:

\begin{equation}
M_{60} = \frac{P_{60} - P_{\text{exec}}}{P_{\text{exec}}} \times 10,000 \text{ (in basis points)}
\end{equation}

where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $P_{\text{exec}}$ = Execution price on DEX
    \item $P_{60}$ = CEX reference price 60 seconds after execution
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Interpretation}: Positive markout indicates DEX execution was better than CEX.

\subsection{Statistical Tests}

\subsubsection{Two-Sample t-Test}

For comparing means between groups A and B:

\begin{equation}
t = \frac{\bar{X}_A - \bar{X}_B}{\sqrt{\frac{s_A^2}{n_A} + \frac{s_B^2}{n_B}}}
\end{equation}

Significance levels: * $p < 0.10$, ** $p < 0.05$, *** $p < 0.01$

\subsubsection{Difference-in-Differences (DiD)}

For the FCBA natural experiment:

\begin{equation}
\text{DiD} = (\bar{Y}_{\text{CoW,post}} - \bar{Y}_{\text{CoW,pre}}) - (\bar{Y}_{\text{1inch,post}} - \bar{Y}_{\text{1inch,pre}})
\end{equation}

Standard error:
\begin{equation}
SE_{\text{DiD}} = \sqrt{\frac{s^2_{\text{CoW,post}}}{n} + \frac{s^2_{\text{CoW,pre}}}{n} + \frac{s^2_{\text{1inch,post}}}{n} + \frac{s^2_{\text{1inch,pre}}}{n}}
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI)}

For solver concentration:

\begin{equation}
HHI = \sum_{i=1}^{N} s_i^2 \times 10,000
\end{equation}

where $s_i$ is solver $i$'s market share. HHI $> 2,500$ indicates high concentration.

\section{Summary of Key Findings}

\subsection{Robust Findings (Consistent Across Periods)}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{OFA execution quality is economically significant}: +2.0 to +4.0 bps vs CEX, consistent with Bachu et al. (2024) benchmark of 4--5 bps

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Native ETH premium: Significant only in P2--P3
    \item USDT vs USDC premium: Strong in P1--P3, disappears in P4
    \item BUY vs SELL advantage: Direction reverses across periods
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Null Findings}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{FCBA mechanism change}: DiD = $-0.11$ bps (not significant)
    \item \textbf{Large trade advantage from FCBA}: DiD = $-0.13$ bps (not significant)
\end{enumerate}
